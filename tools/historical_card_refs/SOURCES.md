# Historical unit-card references

## French Chassepot infantry pilot

- File: `french_chassepot_soldier_carjat.jpg`
- Subject: French soldier holding a Chassepot rifle
- Photographer: Étienne Carjat (died 1906)
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soldier-chassepot.jpg
- Licence: Public domain / Public Domain Mark 1.0
- Use: authoritative pose, anatomy, hand placement, and grounded-rifle interaction for the French Fusiliers pilot. It is a Chassepot rather than the late unit's Lebel, so it is not an exact weapon reference; the verified Steam & Steel card/model controls the uniform, late weapon configuration, colours, and card style.

Each unit-reference source is assigned to one internal unit only. Approved period kneeling references in `tools/historical_skirmisher_pose_sources.json` may be reused solely to control skirmisher/sharpshooter pose and weapon handling; every such unit still requires its own distinct uniform/equipment reference. Context-only photographs without a clear unit subject are rejected; the discarded Cuirassiers-at-Metz camp photograph, for example, did not establish a mounted pose.
